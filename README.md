## <img src="https://github.com/KrishBharadwaj5678/Astralis/raw/main/readme/gifs/howItWorks.gif" width="35"/> How It Works

### 1️⃣ Download the Images

- **Download the images** from the repository's `markers` folder to your device.

### 2️⃣ Download the App

- [Download the app](https://drive.google.com/file/d/17-OUs8Y3uuJZaItPMiBOmMp69okh2GfS/view?usp=sharing) using the link provided in the repository.

### 3️⃣ Launch the App

- **Open the app** to begin the immersive augmented reality experience.

### 4️⃣ Scan the Image

- **Scan any downloaded image** using the app to activate the AR experience.

### 5️⃣ Experience in AR

- **View the planets in immersive 3D** and experience it through augmented reality.
