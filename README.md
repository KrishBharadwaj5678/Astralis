# 🌌 Welcome to Astralis 🌌

**✨ Bring Space to Life in Augmented Reality! ✨**  
Explore the cosmos like never before with **stunning 3D models** of planets, rovers, and asteroids that come alive when you scan their images. Dive into the universe and interact with celestial wonders—all from your device!  

---

### 🚀 **Getting Started is Easy!** 🚀  

Follow these simple steps to embark on your space adventure with SpaceExplorer AR:  

---

### **Step 1:**  
🖼️ **Download the celestial images** provided in the repository to your device.  

---

### **Step 2:**  
📲 **Download the app** using the link provided.  

---

### **Step 3:**  
🎮 **Open the app** and prepare for an exciting journey through space!  

---

### **Step 4:**  
📸 **Scan any of the celestial images** you downloaded to summon detailed 3D models of planets, rovers, and asteroids.  

---

### **Step 5:**  
🌟 **Explore and interact with the 3D models** in your space with immersive AR visuals.  

---

Embark on an unforgettable journey and bring the wonders of space exploration right to your fingertips!
